# Rolling the Pitch

The boxed version of the game played on this site.

Two engraved dice, a pad of scoresheets, a rulebook, and a deck of **blank
character cards**. The cards are blank on purpose. The whole point of the game
is that you decide who is batting, and the best version of it is the one played
by two people who have just argued about whether Napoleon would open.

## What is in the box

- Two dice, batting and bowling
- Fifty scoresheets
- A rulebook, including the dice profiles used in The Pinnacle League
- Forty blank character cards and a pencil

## Where it came from

The mechanic is a descendant of pencil cricket, played in Britain since before
the war and sold commercially from 1924. Nobody owns it, which is as it should
be. What is new here is the character profiles, which turn a game of pure
chance into a competition with a record book.
