# How it works

Two dice, five wickets, and no skill whatsoever. The game is a descendant of
pencil cricket, played in Britain since before the war.

## The batting die

1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 are runs. A 5 is an appeal.

## The bowling die

1 bowled, 2 caught, 3 lbw, 4 run out, 5 not out, 6 stumped.

## The match

Five wickets an innings and two innings each side, like a Test. The follow-on
is available at a lead of sixty. The side batting fourth stops the moment it
passes the target.

## Why some players behave oddly

Every character in this competition has a dice profile of their own. None of
them are published. Work them out from the record book.
